The problem

Farmers feed the country but stay outside its financial system

39%

of adults are financially included

43%

of jobs are in agriculture

~30%

of GDP comes from agriculture

2.5M+

active mobile-money wallets, yet cash still dominates rural buying

Paid in cash, no records, no savings for planting season, no credit history. Buyers carry risky cash to remote buying points.

Sources: Global Findex 2024 and wallet data as cited in the TOR; World Bank (employment 2023, GDP share 2025)

Our insight

The harvest payment is the best moment to bring a farmer into formal finance.

Make that payment digital, and savings, records and credit follow. The buyer, who already wants to stop handling cash, becomes the channel.

The solution

One platform, four connected parts

① Buyer / Agent app

Android, offline-first. Record weight, grade and price. Instant payout to Orange Money, Afrimoney or QMoney.

② Farmer USSD

*384*55# on any phone, in English or Krio. Payments, savings, expenses, FarmScore, loan offers.

③ Co-op dashboard

Replaces paper buying books. Live volumes, payouts, savings, women's participation, audit reports.

④ Lender portal

Consent-based, explainable FarmScore. Send input-loan offers to the farmer's phone. Repaid automatically at harvest.

TOR use cases covered: (a) digital payments · (b) savings · (d) credit scoring · (f) record keeping · (g) digital transformation. Insurance is added in Year 2.

Credit scoring

FarmScore: explainable, consent-based credit

A 300–850 score from alternative data:

• Digital sales history and income
• Savings behaviour
• Co-op membership tenure
• Repayment record
• Input-expense records

Safeguards: no gender, ethnicity or religion factors. Revocable consent on USSD. Farmers see their own score. Gender bias audit during the pilot.

Insurance

Commission from a licensed insurer, premium collected at point of sale

Unit economics: a 500-farmer co-op brings in ≈ NLe 59,000 a year at a ≈ 63% contribution margin, and saves the buyer more than that in cash-handling costs.
